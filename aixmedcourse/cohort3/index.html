FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior AI or machine learning experience?
No. You only need to be comfortable coding in Python. We start from the fundamentals and build up to advanced medical-imaging models across the ten sessions.
What level of Python is expected?
You should be comfortable with variables, functions, loops, and using libraries. Every session is hands-on, so you'll be writing code from day one. This is not a beginner programming course.
When exactly does the bootcamp run?
On weekdays from January 5 to 18, 2027, with one 3-hour session per day (30 hours total). Exact daily times are shared with registered participants.
Is it online or in person?
This cohort is fully in person at The Submarine Coworking Space in Beirut. See the map.
What do I need to bring?
Your own laptop. Setup instructions for Python and the tools we use are sent before the first session.
How do I pay, and when is my seat confirmed?
After you register, we email payment instructions. Your seat is confirmed once payment is received. There are only 8 seats, allocated first come, first served.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes. You receive a completion certificate at the end of the bootcamp.
