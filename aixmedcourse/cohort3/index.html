Curriculum

From zero to hero

Seven stages that build on each other, from your first classifier to the models behind today's medical AI, capped by a capstone project.

STAGE 01

SVM

Support vector machines and classical ML, hands-on with Scikit-Learn.

STAGE 02

Neural Networks

Perceptrons, backpropagation, and training dynamics from the ground up.

STAGE 03

CNNs

Convolutional networks for extracting features from medical images.

STAGE 04

UNets

Encoder–decoder architectures for medical image segmentation.

STAGE 05

Transformers

Vision transformers for imaging tasks.

STAGE 06

Vision–Language Alignment

Connecting images and text with models like BioMedCLIP.

STAGE 07

Self-Supervised Learning

Representation learning without labels, the frontier of medical AI.

AND MORE

Many more topics

Plus additional models, tools, and techniques covered throughout the bootcamp.

CAPSTONE

Your Project

Apply the full pipeline to a real imaging problem, data to model.
